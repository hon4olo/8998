# Structural sources and boundaries — October 14, 2026

The existing12.4s thyroid scene is revised in place. Narration is unchanged: source28.72–41.12s. This authorizes the revision workflow; it does not imply user acceptance of the final art.

## Downloaded, inspected, retained

- [PDB2H77](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2H77): human thyroid hormone receptor alpha, chainA, T3-bound ligand-binding domain; X-ray2.33Å. Nascimento et al., J Mol Biol360,586(2006), DOI10.1016/j.jmb.2006.05.008. Coordinate file includes engineered/crystallographic details, modified cysteines and unresolved residues. We render255 observed alpha carbons; split curves at numbering gaps. We do not invent missing residues, the complete receptor, a DNA-binding domain or a receptor/DNA pose.
- [PDB1Y0X](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1Y0X): thyroxine–thyroid receptor beta interaction,3.10Å. Used as a structural reference for T4/receptor context, not mixed into the rendered alpha-receptor fold.
- [PDB1BSX](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1BSX): TR beta/T3/coactivator complex,3.70Å; consulted, not the rendered fold.
- [CCD T3](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand/T3): atom identities, bond connectivity/orders, formulaC15H12I3NO4. T3 coordinates are the bound ligand from2H77, not hand-drawn geometry.
- [CCD T44](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand/T44): thyroxine, formulaC15H11I4NO4. Render uses the supplied CCD ideal conformer, rigidly aligned to T3 by chemically corresponding atoms. Thus the film does not suggest both conformations came from one crystal.

Raw official PDB/CIF files and SHA-256 hashes in assets/structures/. `scripts/prepare-thyroid-structures.py` parses the files and produces geometry.json. The dictionary names, graph mapping and iodine counts are auditable. Common atom mapping preserves chemical connectivity. Extra outer-ring iodineI5′ is removed conceptually during T4→T3; all hydrogens are omitted. Intermediate coordinates are artistic interpolation, **not a measured reaction trajectory**. Deiodinase/reaction chemistry and kinetics are not simulated.

## Real geometry versus artistic choices

**Data-derived:**23/24 ligand heavy atoms, their chemical connections,255 observed Cα positions, ligand bound pose, and2027 protein heavy atoms used for a partial space-filling view. CAS arsenic and attached CE1/CE2 adduct atoms are omitted for clarity; ordinary residues' CE1/CE2 atoms are retained. Secondary structure is annotated from PDB HELIX records. Ribbon width and spline interpolation are visual representations of the trace, not atomistic surfaces. Space-filling radius is0.82×standard element van der Waals radius, not a computed solvent-accessible surface.

**Artistic:**satin/copper palette, atom size/style, lighting, camera, tissue cells, cutaway plane, ligand approach trajectory and conversion interpolation. Surface removal reveals the backbone and pocket; it is a graphical section, not protein opening or unfolding. Nothing deforms the receptor fold. Final gene-expression view is an idealized right-handed double helix (no exact groove geometry), with no specified sequence. It is a separate editorial cut, not a fabricated complex with the ligand-binding domain. The picture implies regulation, not universal activation of every gene.

No molecular structure was generated by an image model. No background music, synthetic speech or time stretching. No new clinical/statistical claims.

## Rendering decision

Three.js0.180.0 provides real mesh lighting, perspective, depth buffering, instancing and clipping inside the existing Canvas compositor. Chromium supports WebGL2 through ANGLE/Vulkan SwiftShader on CPU. No physical GPU was used. Existing `seek(t)`, Playwright frame export and FFmpeg encoding are unchanged. Blender4.3.2 was discovered but not used or benchmarked; browser WebGL avoids introducing a separate offline render/cache workflow.

Molecular material colors are an art palette, not a quantitative map of electrostatic potential, binding energy or electron density. Readability and aesthetics must not be confused with scientific validation.
