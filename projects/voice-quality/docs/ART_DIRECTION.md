# Thyroid signal — quality-test scene

12.4 seconds, 1080×1920 at 30fps. Original source 28.72–41.12 seconds. This is the requested artistic test, not the complete ZNAYA film.

| Scene time | Spoken anchor | Visual action and purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.9 | «В разных тканях часть его» | Sculpted cell membranes with parallax; camera enters the tissue. T4 context is established by the next state. |
| 1.9–5.9 | «превращается в более активный Т3» | One molecular motif retains its body while one of four iodine nodes detaches; T4 changes to T3 at 3.96. Camera follows the remaining molecule. |
| 5.9–10.26 | «Т3 связывается… рецепторами» | Ligand approaches the folded receptor and settles into a pocket during «связывается». Receptor sits above DNA inside a schematic nuclear space. Hold allows the long phrase to read. |
| 10.26–12.4 | «влияет на экспрессию генов» | Shared receptor/DNA camera pivots and closes in; a bounded response region gains copper contrast. The mechanism reads as modulation, without claiming universal gene activation. |

Material grammar: petroleum-dark space, ivory cell surfaces and protein ribbons, copper iodine and response accents. Front/back occlusion, shaded spheres, lamellae and deterministic microtexture give volume without WebGL. The typography is left aligned and subordinate to the molecular narrative; large T4/T3 labels resolve the pronoun in the excerpt.

This is a schematic scientific illustration, not a solved protein structure, precise chemical diagram or literal tissue anatomy. Deiodinase is not shown, and the reaction is compressed conceptually. The DNA connection is a visual relationship, not an experimental binding pose. Review scientific details and acquire approved brand typography before using this as final production footage.

Existing Canvas renderer, Playwright screenshots and FFmpeg remain unchanged. No music or SFX were added. A spatial shading mask reserves text contrast during the final close-up; it does not fade between shots. New geometry lives only in scenes/thyroid-quality.ts.
